Help & FAQ ▶
What are unreconciled transactions? ▶
Unreconciled transactions are bank transactions in Xero that haven't been matched to an invoice, bill, or categorised yet. Until they're reconciled, your books aren't fully up to date.
How do I reconcile in Xero? ▶
Click Open in Xero on any account row to go directly to that account's reconciliation page. Alternatively, in Xero go to Accounting → Bank Accounts and click Reconcile next to the account. Match each transaction to an existing record or create a new one.
The transaction count looks wrong ▶
This app only fetches authorised, unreconciled bank transactions. Draft transactions, deleted transactions, and already-reconciled items are excluded by design. Click ↺ Refresh (or press R) to get the latest data from Xero.
My session expired — what do I do? ▶
Xero access tokens expire after 30 minutes. The app will try to refresh your session automatically. If you see a "session expired" message, click Disconnect and then Open Xero Login to reconnect. Your Client ID and Secret are saved — you only need to re-authorise.
Is my data safe? ▶
Yes. This app uses Xero's official OAuth2 PKCE flow — your Xero password is never seen or stored. OAuth tokens are held only in sessionStorage (they're wiped when you close the tab). Your Client ID and Secret are stored in your browser's local storage only, and are never sent anywhere except to Xero's own servers.
Can I use this with multiple Xero organisations? ▶
Yes. If your Xero account has access to multiple organisations, you'll be prompted to choose one after connecting. You can switch organisations at any time using the organisation badge in the footer.
